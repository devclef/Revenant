# Requirements Document

Spec: 20 Easier sealing and receipts

## Introduction

Some people can't finish sealing today, and some people think the letter is gone at the end.

- **The press is hard to find.** The Seal_Step's only seal control is the Press_Envelope. With full motion it needs a 900 ms hold. A short mouse click lets the wax recede and starts nothing.
- **A device without passkeys has no way in before it fails.** The Seal_Step only offers "Seal with a backup key instead" when the PRF hint is missing, and SealPage never passes the handler, so that button never renders on the stepper. The backup key path is reachable only from the `cannot_lock` screen, after a failed passkey prompt.
- **A work PC that blocks passkeys can't try the live demo.** There is no way to rehearse the two-press ceremony without a working platform passkey.
- **The receipts hide the letter.** At stage 3 with figures, `Reveal.tsx` moves the letter into a closed "Read the letter again" disclosure after the figures. After "See the receipts" or "Show everything", readers think the letter has gone.

This spec has four parts. Parts C and D stand on their own and can ship first. Parts A and B build on the Seal_Step that Part C leaves, but Part C does not need them.

- **Part C (Requirements 1 and 2).** A plain Seal_Button under the Press_Envelope does exactly what a completed hold does, in demo and real mode.
- **Part D (Requirements 3 to 6).** At stage 3 with figures, the letter stays open. At the Wide_Width it sits in its own column beside the receipts and stays in view while they scroll.
- **Part A (Requirements 7 to 9).** The Seal_Step always offers "Seal with a backup key instead". It seals with the Backup_Wrap only, with no passkey prompt, in demo and real mode.
- **Part B (Requirements 10 to 14).** On `/demo/live` only, an opt-in Simulated_Mode runs the same two presses through an in-memory Simulated_Port. The backup key is required in that mode. It is never reachable on `/seal`.

Requirements 15 to 17 apply to every part.

Nothing in the sealed format, the routes, the database, the Helm chart or the environment variables changes. Part A changes no file under `src/lib/crypto/`. Part B adds one production module for the Simulated_Port, exported from the crypto barrel `src/lib/crypto/index.ts` by added lines only, and changes no existing crypto code (Requirements 10.8 and 15.2).

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: A plain Seal_Button beside the wax (Part C)

**User Story:** As someone sealing a letter with a mouse, I want a plain button that seals, so that I can finish even when I don't know to hold the wax.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE the Seal_Step shows its seal form (Seal_State `write`, `creating`, `created`, `confirming`, `preparing` or `sealing`), THE Seal_Step SHALL render exactly one Seal_Button, in Demo_Mode and in Real_Mode: a native `button` element with `type="button"`, the shared primary button class (`buttons.module.css` `primary`) and a `data-seal-button` attribute, placed inside `[data-press-column]` as a later sibling of the Press_Envelope's wrapper and an earlier sibling of the press list (`SealSteps`). WHILE the Seal_State is `cannot_lock`, `cancelled`, `failed`, `fallback_key` or `sealed`, THE Seal_Page SHALL render no Seal_Button.
2. WHEN the Seal_Button is activated by a mouse click, a touch tap, Enter or Space while the Press_Controller is in `write` mode and the Seal_Step is not Press_Blocked, THE Seal_Step SHALL call `onCreatePress` exactly once. The activation SHALL go through the same Press_Controller instance as the Press_Envelope, as one `ACTIVATE` event, whatever the click's `detail` value and whatever the motion setting (full, reduced or Calm).

### Requirement 10: The Simulated_Port (Part B)

**User Story:** As a judge on a work PC that blocks passkeys, I want a stand-in for the passkey that works in this tab, so that I can rehearse the two presses.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Simulated_Port SHALL implement `AuthenticatorPort` from `src/lib/crypto/authenticator-port.ts` with no change to that type. `isAvailable()` SHALL return true synchronously on every call, and SHALL read neither `navigator.credentials` nor `PublicKeyCredential`.
2. WHEN a Simulated_Port is created, THE Simulated_Port SHALL draw a 32-byte secret from `crypto.getRandomValues`, import it with `crypto.subtle.importKey` as an HMAC key with hash SHA-256, `extractable` false and the usage `sign` only, and, once the import has resolved, overwrite every byte of its own copy of the raw secret with 0. It SHALL hold no reference to the raw bytes after that.
3. WHEN `create(options)` is called, THE Simulated_Port SHALL draw a new 32-byte `credentialId` from `crypto.getRandomValues`, remember it for this instance, and return `{ credentialId, prfResult }`, where `prfResult` is the 32-byte HMAC-SHA256(secret, `options.prfSalt`) computed with `crypto.subtle.sign` only. It SHALL ignore every other field of `options`, SHALL make no `navigator.credentials` call, and SHALL return byte copies that a caller can change without changing the port's state.
4. WHEN `get(options)` is called and at least one `options.allowCredentialIds` entry is byte-equal (same length, same bytes) to a `credentialId` this instance returned from `create`, THE Simulated_Port SHALL return the first such entry as `credentialId` and the 32-byte HMAC-SHA256(secret, `options.prfSalt`) as `prfResult`, with no `navigator.credentials` call. It SHALL not check `options.rpId`; the hostname check stays in `assertPasskey`.
5. IF `get(options)` is called with an empty `allowCredentialIds` or with no entry byte-equal to a `credentialId` this instance returned, THEN THE Simulated_Port SHALL reject with `PortError("failed")`, SHALL compute no PRF value, and SHALL make no `navigator.credentials` call.
6. FOR ALL 32-byte salts, THE Simulated_Port SHALL return byte-equal 32-byte `prfResult` values from `create` and from a following `get` for the returned credential, so `confirmPasskey` returns `ok: true` with no `no_prf` or `prf_mismatch` (property, fast-check, at least 100 runs). FOR ALL pairs of Simulated_Port instances whose secrets differ, THE `prfResult` values for the same salt SHALL differ (property, at least 100 runs, secrets from a seeded stub of `crypto.getRandomValues`). With `crypto.getRandomValues` stubbed to give a known secret, `prfResult` SHALL equal an HMAC-SHA256 that the test computes on its own from that secret and salt (known-answer test).
7. THE Simulated_Port SHALL write nothing to `localStorage`, `sessionStorage`, IndexedDB, cookies, the URL, `history`, the console or the network, and its only own enumerable properties SHALL be `isAvailable`, `create` and `get`. No method, property, getter, error message or `toString` result SHALL contain the secret, the raw key bytes or the `CryptoKey`. A test passes this criterion when it enumerates the instance's own and prototype properties and finds only those three, and the storage scan of Requirement 15.6 finds none of the forbidden APIs in the module.
8. THE Simulated_Port SHALL be a production module under `src/lib/crypto/`, outside `src/lib/crypto/testing/`, exported from the crypto barrel `src/lib/crypto/index.ts`. It SHALL not import `src/lib/crypto/testing/fake-authenticator.ts`, and `no-fake-import.test.ts` SHALL keep passing with no change to its rule.
9. THE Seal_Page SHALL reach the Simulated_Port only through the crypto module it already loads with `import()`, or through another module it loads with `import()`, with no static value import of `@/lib/crypto` (spec 19 R7.6). `seal-page-imports.test.ts` SHALL keep passing.
10. WHEN the Seal_Page that created a Simulated_Port unmounts, THE Seal_Page SHALL drop its reference to that Simulated_Port. A test passes this criterion when two mounts of the Seal_Page in Simulated_Mode, with the same salt, give different `prfResult` values from their ports.
11. IF `crypto.subtle.importKey` or `crypto.subtle.sign` rejects inside the Simulated_Port, THEN THE Simulated_Port SHALL reject the pending creation, `create` or `get` with `PortError("failed")` and no browser error text, SHALL return no `credentialId` or PRF value, and SHALL hold no raw secret bytes afterwards.

## Decisions this spec makes beyond the request

The owner may strike any of these before design starts.

- **Backup-only sealing stays on the Backup_Fallback screen.** The request says the Seal_Button runs "that path's press" on the backup-only path, labelled "Seal the letter". The Backup_Fallback screen has no Press_Envelope, and its seal control is already a plain primary button ("Seal with this key"). This spec keeps that button as the backup-only press and adds no Seal_Button there, so Part C does not depend on Part A. `LIVE_COPY.noPrfPress` is changed to name that button (Requirement 7.9).
- **Labels.** The Seal_Button reads "Make your passkey" and "Set the seal", the same words as the press list under it. Simulated_Mode reads "Make the simulated passkey" on the first press.
- **`fixFields` on the Seal_Step.** The request expects the fix-fields line on the Seal_Step. Today it shows only on the `all` form. Requirement 2.3 shows it on the Seal_Step too.
- **No Signet animation is added.** The Seal_Step has no Signet motion today (`pose` is never set). Requirement 1.7 asks for the pose a completed hold leaves, which is the full Wax_Pool.
- **Backup_Only_Start needs no Backup_Choice.** The Backup_Choice decides only whether a passkey letter also gets a backup wrap.
- **`noPrfOther` only after a failure.** "Other options: try another device …" reads as a failure line, so it shows only when the Backup_Fallback came from `cannot_lock`.
- **Simulated_Letters carry both wraps.** The header still has a passkey wrap (`credentialId`, `prfSalt`), because the in-page unlock uses it. On the Letter_Page that wrap can't be used, and the backup key opens the letter.
- **The Simulated_Port lives under `src/lib/crypto/`** (crypto-dev), as the one WebCrypto module next to `browserAuthenticatorPort`. It is exported from the crypto barrel `src/lib/crypto/index.ts` (Requirement 10.8, the only modified crypto file allowed by Requirement 15.2), and the Seal_Page reaches it only through the crypto module it loads with `import()` (Requirements 10.9 and 13.3).
- **The secret lives as long as the Seal_Page instance**, not the browser tab, so leaving `/demo/live` and coming back starts a new secret.
- **The simulated Wait step shows the backup key card open**, since it is the only lasting way in.
- **Receipts layout.** Two columns only at the Wide_Width (900 px, the repo's existing wide breakpoint). Record_Column at the inline start, Letter_Column at the inline end. The document order is the same at every width: question pairs, letter, figures, seal line, end card. 768×1024 is one column.
- **Letters with no figures keep today's layout** at every width, since their last stage is the Read_Stage.

## Open questions for the owner

- **Letter_Page notice for a Simulated_Letter.** A passkey attempt on `/l/<id>` shows "Nothing was opened. Press the button to try again.", which invites a loop. A line pointing at the backup key field for letters with both wraps would need a `UnlockView` change. This spec leaves the Letter_Page unchanged (Requirement 14.7).
- **`REAL_COPY.intro` with the Backup_Only_Start.** The Real_Mode intro says "Your letter is sealed with your own passkey." A backup-only real letter is not. A neutral rewrite is new copy for the owner.
- **Two h1 elements on the demo Backup_Fallback screen.** Demo_Mode renders the temporary "Press the seal" h1 and the screen's own h1, as it does for `cannot_lock` today. Real_Mode does not. Requirement 7.7 needs a focus target on the screen heading. The design may fix the demo heading rule or leave it.
- **Letter_Column scroll on very long letters.** A 5,000-character letter in a sticky column with its own scroll is a nested scroll area. The alternative is a sticky column with no inner scroll that simply scrolls away for long letters.
